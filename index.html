How the odds are calculated

1 · Rarity roll

Every appraisal first rolls a rarity: R5 at 59%, R6 at 27%, R7 at 11%, R8 at 3%. Most dream talismans can only come from one or two of these.

2 · Template roll

Each rarity has fixed templates (29 in total) that decide which skill pools the talisman draws from — for example, a weapon-skill tier-3 slot plus a 3-slot armor skill. If no template can hold your combination, the talisman cannot exist.

3 · Skill & slot rolls

Each slot then draws one skill uniformly from its pool (9–43 entries depending on the pool), and decoration slots roll one of the template’s patterns. Multiply it all together and you get the exact odds shown above.

Data: the game’s datamined talisman tables (skill pools, 288 entries; templates, 29 rows), cross-checked against our own save-editing dataset. Templates within a rarity are weighted equally — relative template weights have never been published, and every public calculator makes the same assumption. R8 slot patterns use the datamined 50 / 33 / 17 split.
